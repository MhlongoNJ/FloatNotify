Float Notify

Privacy Policy · Effective October 31, 2026

Float Notify (the “App”) is published by Cyber Terrorist Dragon (“we”, “us”). The App shows a floating button that opens your notification drawer and offers quick actions. This policy explains what the App does with your information.

The short version: Float Notify has no accounts, no servers and no analytics of our own. It does not read the content of your screen or your notifications. Your settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what the Google AdMob advertising SDK collects to show ads (see “Advertising” below).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or transmit personal information on any server, and we do not require you to create an account. We do not sell your data.

2. Permissions and how they are used

Accessibility service

  • Used only to perform actions you choose from the floating button: opening the notification drawer or Quick Settings, taking a screenshot, locking the screen, opening the power menu, Recents, Home and Back.
  • The service receives window-change events so the App can tell which app is on screen. It uses only the app’s package name, to hide the button in apps you selected and to apply per-app gesture profiles you created. The package name is kept in memory only.
  • The service is configured so that it cannot read the text or content shown on your screen. We do not record, store or share anything you type or view.

Notification access (optional)

  • If you grant it, the App counts your unread notifications to show a number on the button and to animate it when a new notification arrives.
  • The App does not read, store or transmit the title, text or any other content of your notifications. Only the number of notifications is used, and it is held in memory.
  • You can turn this off at any time in Android Settings → Notifications → Notification access.

Internet and network state

  • Used only so that advertisements can be loaded. The App has no other online features.

Flashlight

  • The optional flashlight action switches the device torch on and off. The App does not access the camera, photos or video.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores your preferences locally: button size, theme, gesture mappings, the list of apps where the button is hidden, per-app gesture profiles and the button’s screen position. This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself (see “Backups”). It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

4. Backups you create

The App lets you save or share a backup file of your settings. The file contains your settings and the package names of apps you hid or created profiles for. It contains no notification content and no personal information. Where the file goes (a folder, a message, an email) is entirely your choice, and we never receive it.

5. Advertising

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you are in a region where consent is required, you will be asked for your choice. Ads are never shown from the floating button or over other apps.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google, through the AdMob SDK as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no user data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the App stores on your device, uninstall it or clear its storage in Android settings. Data held by Google for advertising is managed through your Google account’s ad settings.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Because settings are stored only on your device and we operate no servers for the App, we have no central store of user data to protect. Ad requests are made over encrypted connections by the AdMob SDK.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Since we do not hold your personal data, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through your account settings. You can also contact us using the details below.

11. App Rating

The App may occasionally invite you to rate it using Google Play’s in-app review feature. Reviews are handled entirely by Google Play, and we receive no personal information from this prompt.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and significant changes will be reflected in the App’s store listing.
